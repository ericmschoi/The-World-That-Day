Privacy policy

This page explains what information The World That Day ("this service") receives and how it uses it.

Operator: The World That Day operator

What we receive

This service receives the date of birth (a date) and place of birth that you enter. It does not receive accounts, names or contact details. Right now only the date is sent to the server; the place will be received once place input is added.

How we use and share it

  • We use the information we receive only to find photos, videos and events for that day and place. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • This service's server asks outside sources for material by date and event name. Right now these are Korean Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons (photos and videos) and YouTube Data API (video search on the KTV Archive channel). When a source for resolving places is added, it will be added to this policy.
  • Outside sources receive the date as a search term. They are not told whose date it is.
  • Results for each date (Wikipedia passages and the like) are cached on the server, but information about the person who asked (such as the IP address) is not stored.
  • We have no accounts and keep no record that links a date or place to you. Results are cached by date and event, not by person.
  • The server's work records (logs) may contain dates and related page titles, but never a date of birth together with a place.
  • The address of the result page never contains your date of birth or place.

Content from elsewhere and ads

Your browser loads the photos and videos on the result page directly from their original source's servers (Wikimedia Commons and others). Those servers then receive connection information (such as your IP address). YouTube videos are shown by placing the YouTube player (privacy-enhanced mode) in the page, and YouTube may show ads in the player.

Wikimedia (servers in the United States) and Google/YouTube receive your IP address and device details under their own privacy policies. Your information may be processed in other countries, where authorities may be able to access it.

YouTube API Services

This service uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles personal information is set out in the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and device storage

This service does not use cookies or browser storage. However, the YouTube player and the YouTube IFrame API script may store or read information on your device, even in privacy-enhanced mode.

Contact

We hold nothing that can be looked up by name or account, so there is nothing to access or correct. Questions or complaints: open an issue at GitHub Issues. Do not post personal information in a public issue. Responsible person: the operator.

Effective date and changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and write a new effective date.
